The Gap

Cities are flying blind between complaints.

Fixed CCTV covers junctions, not the 150km of road between them. Manual inspection is slow and sparse. Citizen complaints arrive after the pothole has already caused an accident. Buses drive every road, every day — and their cameras are only ever used after the fact.

01 / BLIND SPOTS

Fixed cameras miss most of the network

CCTV and static sensors sit at junctions and arterial roads. Interior streets, service lanes and outer routes go unmonitored between scheduled inspections.

02 / LATENCY

Response lags behind reality

A pothole reported by a citizen today may already be a week old. Rash driving and hit-and-run incidents are rarely captured with enough evidence to act on.

03 / UNUSED FOOTAGE

Bus cameras record but don't sense

Every bus already carries front, rear, side and cabin cameras. Today that footage is pulled only after an incident — never analysed as it's captured.

How Drishti Works

From dashcam to decision, in four stages.

An edge-AI pipeline onboard every bus, feeding a centralized platform that turns raw footage into city-wide situational awareness — without saturating the network.

STAGE 01

Detect

Onboard edge AI reads every camera feed live — road surface, traffic, and cabin views — without sending raw video anywhere.

  • Road defect & hazard detection
  • Vehicle detection, class & count
  • Pedestrian & school-zone risk
STAGE 02

Transmit

Only structured events leave the bus — a tag, a confidence score, a timestamp, a GPS fix. Not the video.

  • Compressed event payloads
  • GPS + timestamp on every alert
  • Encrypted uplink to command server
STAGE 03

Analyze

The central platform aggregates the whole fleet into one live picture of the city's roads.

  • GIS event mapping
  • Congestion heat maps
  • Origin–destination patterns
STAGE 04

Act

Transport authorities get a prioritized, evidence-backed queue instead of a pile of complaints.

  • Maintenance work orders
  • Route delay estimates
  • Incident reports for enforcement

Why Edge, Not Cloud-First

Bandwidth is the real constraint — so intelligence lives on the bus.

Streaming raw multi-camera video from 100+ buses would flood any city network. Drishti processes video on the vehicle and only ever transmits what matters: a class, a confidence score, a coordinate, a time.
