THE INFRASTRUCTURE PROBLEM & THE DRISHTI PARADIGM

Why Traditional Road Audits Fail — And How Bus-Borne AI Solves It

cancel The Traditional Approach (Broken & Reactive) Inefficient
  • • High CapEx: Dedicated inspection vehicles cost ₹80L–₹1.5Cr each and sit idle most of the year.
  • • Infrequent Surveys: Roads are surveyed only once every 6–12 months. By then, micro-cracks become fatal craters.
  • • Reactive Citizen Complaints: Municipalities learn of road hazards only after fatal accidents or public outrage.
  • • Slow Paper Bureaucracy: 3–4 weeks delay between defect discovery, PWD inspection, and contractor tender issuance.
check_circle The दृष्टि Autonomous AI Approach (Living Sensing Mesh) 100x Scalable
  • • Zero New Fleet Costs: Piggybacks on public transit buses already traversing every city street 18h a day.
  • • Continuous 20x Daily Scans: Every major corridor is scanned multiple times daily, catching 2cm fissures before craters form.
  • • Multi-Modal AI Fusion: YOLOv8 vision + STAN IMU vibration sensors eliminate shadow false-positives with 97%+ accuracy.
  • • Sub-60s Automated PWD Dockets: Dispatches hot-mix asphalt tonnage, GPS coordinates, and 48-hour contractor SLA automatically.
