Windshield & Road Profilometry

Dual optical setup analyzing front traffic flow and downward pavement roughness to log craters, aggregate unraveling, and pavement dips with millimeter-accurate depth scoring.

ANPR License Plate Intercept

Real-time OCR engine tracking license plates with 97.4% match rate for hit-and-run vector tracking, stolen vehicle intercepts, and automated police PCR alert generation.

Driver DMS & Safety Scoring

60 FPS infrared cabin sensor evaluating Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR), drowsiness triggers, phone distraction, seatbelt compliance, and harsh deceleration events.

Interactive Leaflet mapping with real-time bus pins, spatial hazard clustering, 24-hour timeline scrubber, and ward-level defect filtering across metropolitan corridors.
